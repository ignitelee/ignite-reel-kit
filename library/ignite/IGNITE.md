## 1. What Ignite is

- A trading community with its own free platform: signals, tools, education, an AI coach, live sessions and a
  members community, all in one place (the **Ignite Hub**). Website: **ignitetrading.ai**.
- Lines Ignite uses: "Never trade alone again." "Everything you need to trade." "Trade smart. Live free."
- Founded by **Lee Markham** with his business partner **Alessio**. Lee served 13 years in the RAF, then spent
  5 years building an education business before Ignite.
- Why it exists (Lee): trading has a bad reputation because of who people work with; Ignite was set up as a
  platform that genuinely looks after people. Members who succeed stay, and that's what makes it work.
- Ethos: **"Make the trader win."** Ignite earns when members keep trading, so it needs them to do well
  ("from a business standpoint we need you to win; from a moral standpoint we just want you to win").
- Not the "trader bro" scene (flash cars, rented Lamborghinis, selling the dream): a mature group of
  professionals, many with careers, families and businesses, who treat trading as a skill you learn properly
  and get better at over time.
- What Ignite offers, in Lee's words: education, tools to support your trading, and a community so you're not on
  your own. Nothing is a promise of profit.
- Lee lives in Thailand and takes signals on his phone: at the beach, in a coffee shop, in an airport lounge.

## 2. Free: how it works and how Ignite earns

- Members never pay Ignite: no membership, no subscription, no course to buy. The Hub, signals, Sparkie, the
  Edge, live calls and daily analysis cost nothing.
- To unlock access, a member opens a **live account in their own name with Ignite's partner broker** and funds
  it. That money is their trading capital: it stays in their own broker account, nobody else touches it.
- Partner brokers: Ignite has partnered with two reputable brokers, **PU Prime** (the main one) and
  **Premier Markets**. Members open their account with one of them.
- Joining: sign up to Ignite, get the referral link, create the broker account with it. With the onboarding and
  tutorial videos, people can be set up and trading in hours, not weeks.
- How Ignite earns: the broker shares a small rebate on trading volume with Ignite (Ignite is an affiliate
  partner of the broker). It costs members nothing extra.
- Lee's line: "You can't trade with nothing", so "free" means free from Ignite, not trading without money.
- The deposit is the member's commitment and trading capital, not a payment to Ignite: it stays in their account,
  under their control, and they can withdraw it whenever they like. The tiers make sure everything goes to people
  who intend to use it.

## 3. Signals

- A signal is a professional trader sharing their trade ideas so members can place the same trade on their own
  account. Each signal room is built for a different type of trader, with full training videos (Lee made them).
- Signals arrive in the member's **Telegram** group: direction, entry zone, stop loss and take profits. The member
  places the trade themselves on **MetaTrader 5**: on desktop the Ignite Edge layers the entry into several orders
  with SL and TP preset (they still press the button); on the go they tap it into the MT5 app by hand.
- Ignite has several signal rooms, each built for a different type of trader, and members can switch inside
  the Hub:
  - **Gold** (XAUUSD): steadier calls that give newer traders time to prepare, and **fast gold scalps** (quick
    in, quick out, you need to be at the screen) for experienced traders.
  - **Forex**: fewer calls with larger targets, often held overnight; suits patient traders or anyone short on
    screen time.
- **Never use the names of the signal groups**, and don't state how many groups there are or how many calls a
  day each sends (the website is being updated). Say "signal rooms" or "signal groups".

## 4. The Ignite Hub (free, in the browser; English and Spanish)

- **Gold signals** delivered in your Telegram.
- **Sparkie AI**: reviews your trades (see 5).
- **Ranks & missions**: daily missions reward good habits (respecting your stop, sensible size, no revenge
  trades); a trading leaderboard.
- **Smart alerts / real-time guardrails**: pings you when you drift, e.g. lot size 3x your usual, a revenge-trade
  pattern forming, your own rules bending.
- **On the table**: after you close a winner, shows the move you cut short, so you learn to let winners breathe.
- **Trade journal**: log trades by hand or one tap, pick a reason (FOMO, Revenge, Good patience, Moved SL), add a
  note; the Hub surfaces the pattern you keep repeating.
- **Trade analysis**: every trade a member places is pulled from their MT5 account into their personal Hub
  (via an API), together with the original signal, and the two are compared: where you entered versus the signal,
  what you could have done differently, where you left money on the table. Honest feedback on every trade.
- **Dashboard and trade history**: balance, win rate, P&L, drawdown, every trade with its source (Edge, signal,
  manual), risk & behaviour.
- **Ignite Academy** (education): How to trade the signals, Trading Essentials, Smart Money Concepts, a book
  library, Steve's training, trading room recordings.
- **Calendar** of live sessions; the joining steps (onboarding) inside the Hub.
- Coming soon: **TapIn**, one-tap execution from a signal, sized to your risk rules.

## 5. Sparkie, the AI coach

- An AI trading coach in your pocket, 24 hours a day. Trained on trading psychology and the exact strategies
  behind each signal room. Spelled **Sparkie** (the Hub and website spelling).
- Reads your real trades, live gold conditions and the day's signals, then tells you the truth about your habits.
- Sends a friendly nudge when it matters: move your stop loss and Sparkie sees it ("nothing good happens when you
  move your stop loss"); hit your daily target and it suggests stepping away. It never sleeps and never judges.
- Reviews your actual entries, exits and size (never generic advice); knows what XAUUSD is doing when you ask;
  explains any signal and how to play it; names the patterns costing you, "kindly but honestly".
- Included from the Pro tier.

## 6. Ignite Edge (MT5 Expert Advisor)

- Custom-built for MetaTrader 5 (the app Ignite members trade on) and **exclusive to Ignite**. Enters trades in
  line with the member's signal room.
- One click handles the entry, risk management, partial closes (25% / 50%), break even and a trailing stop; also
  zone orders and a daily target / daily stop, all from one panel.
- **Semi-automated**: nothing fires until you press the button. You stay in control.
- Desktop MT5 only. Included from the Intro tier.

## 7. Ignite Mirror (auto trades)

- Links your own MT5 account to Ignite's master account: when the master trades its strategy, your account places
  the same trades automatically, sized to your balance. A minimum balance check runs first; your money stays in
  your own broker account; pause or disconnect any time.
- Always with its risk line: automated execution can produce losses without your intervention.
- Mirror is Ignite's **auto trades**: the "Auto signals" in the Elite tier.

## 9. The IB (partner) side

- Ignite has two sides: people who want to trade, and **connectors** (IBs) who link people to Ignite.
- You don't need to know how to trade, or trade yourself. You're not selling anything: what you offer is free.
- People join with the IB's referral link, opening their broker account in their own name. When they trade, the
  broker charges a small spread (as anywhere) and shares part of it back with the person who introduced them:
  that's the **rebate**, paid in **US dollars per lot** traded.
- Through Ignite, an IB gets their own contract with the broker at a generous rebate rate.
- **Sub-IBs**: an IB can bring on their own IBs, give them a rate out of their pot and keep the gap, on every level
  below. Example from Lee's video (always labelled as an example): on $10 a lot, give a sub-IB $7, keep $3 on
  every lot their clients trade. The pot is finite because it's paid from real trading activity.
- No ranks, no minimums to hold your position, earn from day one. Rebates are paid daily and can be withdrawn any
  time.
- Partner support, no start-up costs: your own branded Telegram channel and mini app (explains everything and
  pre-qualifies people), the Ignite website with your own referral link, a weekly IB meeting, and weekly
  discovery calls where Ignite does the explaining.
- Suits people who are good with people, care about doing things properly and are ready to take action. Next
  step: book a call with the person who shared it (their partnership manager).

## 10. Words: always, never

**Always**
- On anything about trading or Ignite, a risk line. Approved wording (posters and website):
  - "Trading carries risk and your capital is at risk. Nothing shared is financial advice or a recommendation to
    trade."
  - Short: "Trading involves risk. Only trade with money you can afford to lose."
  - For Mirror / automation: "Automated execution can produce losses without your intervention."
- Label every number that isn't a fixed fact as an example ("An example rate. Yours could differ.").
- Hub screenshots show demo data: say what the Hub tracks, never present them as real results.

**Never**
- Promise or imply income, profits, win rates or "passive income". No "get rich", "guaranteed", "easy money".
- Say how much someone should deposit or invest: Lee can't (FCA rules) and won't. The question to ask is "How much
  could you lose without it hurting your life?"
- Quote the testimonials on the website: they're placeholders, not verified quotes.
- Name the signal groups, or say how many there are.
- Mention the deposit bonus.
- Invent numbers, group names, times, rates or features not in this file.
- Claim "nobody else does this" or that results "transformed": state what the tool does instead.
- Give financial advice. Ignite provides education, tools and analysis.
- Pair lifestyle photos (beach, cafe) with income or results claims.

## 11. Good topics (true and useful)

From Lee's course lessons and videos, safe ground for carousels:
- What the spread is (two prices; the gap is the cost of the trade, like the airport currency rate).
- What a lot size is (how big your trade is; 0.01 is the smallest; get it right and you stay in control).
- Losing trades are normal: trading is probability, nobody wins every trade.
- How much to start with: only what you could lose without it ruining your life.
- Live account vs demo: a demo teaches the buttons, not the emotions (Lee's view).
- How a signal works: zone, stop loss, take profits, layering.
- What's inside the Hub; Sparkie as a coach; the Edge; the live calls; the discovery calls.
- For IBs: how rebates work, sub-IBs, why people use your link (Ignite is the reason they stay).
