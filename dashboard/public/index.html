Help

The answers to the questions people ask most.

Claude keeps asking me to "Allow". Is something wrong?

No, that's normal and it means it's working. Claude asks your permission before it runs anything on your computer (reading your video, cutting it, saving the reel). Click Allow. If it offers to always allow the same thing, choosing that means it asks less next time.

I pasted the message and nothing is happening here

Look at the Claude app: it's probably waiting for you to click Allow. Also check that the folder next to the message box says ignite-reel-kit. The progress bar here only moves once Claude has started.

It's been ages. Is it stuck?

Making reels takes 10 to 40 minutes. Most of that is "Rendering", where the computer builds the video frame by frame, and it can look still for a few minutes. Check the Claude app isn't waiting for an Allow. Keep your computer awake and plugged in.

Does this cost money? Whose account is it using?

It uses your own Claude account and plan, nobody else's. Making reels uses some of your plan's usage, like a long chat. The kit itself is free. Never share your Claude login with anyone, and never use someone else's.

Where are my finished videos?

On the My reels tab: press Download, or Show in folder to see the files. They're saved in the kit's video-projects folder.

I want to change something in a reel

Warning: even a small change takes a long time (often 10 to 20 minutes, longer for long reels), because the whole reel has to be rebuilt and re-rendered. So write all your changes in one go. Under the reel, type what you want changed in normal words, press Make a change message, then paste that into the same Claude chat and press Enter.

How do I add music?

Go to My brand, press Open my music folder, and put MP3 songs into the mood folders (calm, upbeat, inspiring, emotional). Free songs: YouTube Audio Library or Pixabay Music.

I have more than one video, or pictures too

More videos of you talking (you filmed it in parts): add the first in step 1, then press + Add another part of me talking (up to 3 parts; film them in the same place with the same camera so the joins look natural). They're joined into one video in the order shown (use the arrows to change it).

Anything you want SHOWN while you talk (screenshots, photos, phone recordings, short clips): add up to 3 in 1b Extra pictures and clips. For each one, choose How (beside you or full screen) and type When it should appear, e.g. "when I talk about my results". Not sure? Choose "Let Claude decide".

Your logo doesn't go in either: it's in My brand and goes on every reel automatically.

How do I make my reels look more like me?

Use the My library tab. Add photos and clips of your life and work once (your car, office, gym, family, team, results, your own B-roll) with a short note on when to use each. Claude uses the ones that fit in every reel, before any stock footage. The notes are optional: without one, Claude looks at the picture to see what it shows.

Does it post to my Instagram or TikTok?

No. It never posts anything. You download the reel and post it yourself, with the caption from this page.

How do I open this page again?

Double-click IGNITE Reels on your desktop. A small black window opens as well: leave it open while you use the dashboard. Or in Claude, say "open my reel dashboard".

How do I update the kit?

In Claude (Code, in the ignite-reel-kit folder) say: update my IGNITE Reel Kit. Your logo, settings and music are kept.
