In the Claude app

Open the Claude app on your computer (not the website) and click Code at the top. It's the part of Claude that can work with files on your computer. Can't see Code? Update the app, or download it from claude.com/download.

Check the folder

Next to the message box there's a folder name. It should say ignite-reel-kit. If not, click it and choose the ignite-reel-kit folder in your Documents.

Paste and send

Click in the message box, paste (Ctrl + V) and press Enter. Claude starts straight away.

Click Allow (this is normal)

Claude never runs anything on your computer without asking. It asks several times: to read your video, cut it, add captions and graphics, and save the reel. Click Allow. If it offers to always allow the same thing, choose that so it asks less. Nothing is posted online. If it ever asks for something that has nothing to do with your reel, click Deny.

Help

Short answers first, details underneath.

Claude keeps asking me to "Allow". Is something wrong?

No, that's normal. Click Allow.

Claude asks your permission before it runs anything on your computer (reading your video, cutting it, saving the reel). If it offers to always allow the same thing, choose that and it asks less.

What's a trial reel?

An Instagram post that's shown to people who don't follow you yet.

When you share a reel on Instagram, switch on Trial. It goes to new people first, not your followers. That's why we recommend 5 trial versions: post one a day, see which gets the most views, and keep that one on your profile.

What's the hook? What's the cover?

The hook is the first 2 seconds. The cover is the still picture on your profile.

Hook: the words on screen at the start that stop people scrolling. It's already in your video. Under each reel you'll see other openings you could use instead.

Cover: what people see on your profile grid before they press play. When you post, tap "Edit cover" (Instagram) or "Select cover" (TikTok), pick the moment we suggest, and add the cover text.

I pasted the message and nothing is happening

Check the Claude app: it's probably waiting for you to click Allow.

Also check the folder next to Claude's message box says ignite-reel-kit. The progress bar here only moves once Claude has started.

It's been ages. Is it stuck?

Probably not: making reels takes 10 to 40 minutes.

Most of that is "Rendering", where the computer builds the video frame by frame, and it can look still for a few minutes. Check the Claude app isn't waiting for an Allow, and keep your computer awake and plugged in.

Something went wrong. What do I do?

Take a screenshot of the Claude app and send it to whoever shared the kit with you.

On Windows press Windows + Shift + S; on a Mac press Cmd + Shift + 4. Include the last few messages in the Claude chat: they say what happened. You can also ask Claude: "what went wrong, in one sentence?"

Does this cost money? Whose account is it using?

Your own Claude plan. The kit is free.

Making a reel uses some of your plan's usage, like a long chat, and each change uses about as much again. Never share your Claude login, and never use someone else's.

I want to change something in a reel

Write all your changes in one message: each change takes 10 to 20 minutes and uses more of your plan.

Under the reel, open Change something, type what you want in normal words, press Make a change message, and paste it into the same Claude chat. Next time, put more detail in "Anything else?" before you send it.

Where are my finished videos?

On the My reels tab.

Press Download, or Show in folder to see the files (in the kit's video-projects folder).

I have more than one video, or pictures too

More of you talking goes in step 1. Things to show go in step 2.

How do I make my reels look more like me?

Fill your library.

In My library, add photos and clips of your life and work once (car, office, gym, family, team, results). Claude uses the ones that fit in every reel.

How do I add music?

My brand, then "Open my music folder".

Put MP3 songs into the mood folders (calm, upbeat, inspiring, emotional). Free songs: YouTube Audio Library or Pixabay Music.

Does it post to my Instagram or TikTok?

No. It never posts anything.

You download the reel and post it yourself, with the captions from My reels.
